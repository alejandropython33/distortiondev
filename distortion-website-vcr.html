About

Personal builds, not templates.

Distortion Development is a new web and software development company based in the American Midwest, built around a simple idea: every client deserves a site that reflects them, not a recycled layout dragged out of a library.

We specialize in tailored, personal development services — close collaboration, careful listening, and a build process tightly aligned with each client's needs and vision. No bloated agency overhead. No cookie-cutter outputs. Just deliberate, hand-crafted work.

Our development architecture leans heavily on creativity and the personal vision behind each project. We treat the codebase like a creative medium, not just a delivery mechanism — every site we ship has an opinion, a voice, and a reason to exist.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

  • Vision over volume
  • Custom code over templates
  • Client voice drives architecture
  • Craft is a competitive advantage
  • Personal service, every project

How We Work

Transparent terms.

We keep the business side of every project simple, predictable, and written down. No surprise invoices, no hidden fees, no chasing you for renewals or hosting passwords you don't remember. Here's exactly how the engagement works.

// 01

Fixed Project Fees

Every project gets a fixed quote after a discovery call — not an hourly meter that scares you. You know exactly what the build costs before we start.

No surprise invoices. No scope-creep panic. If we agree on the work and the number, that's the number.
// 02

Milestone Payments

Project fees are split into three payments: 30% deposit on signing, 30% at design approval, and 40% at launch.

Spreads the cost over the timeline and protects both sides — you never pay for work that hasn't happened, we never start work that hasn't been committed to.
// 03

Easy Payment Methods

We invoice through Stripe. Pay by ACH bank transfer (preferred for larger invoices), credit card, Apple Pay, or Google Pay — whatever's easiest.

No mailing checks, no awkward Venmo requests. One link, one click, professional invoices in your inbox with PDF receipts.
// 04

Written Contract

Every project starts with a simple service agreement covering scope, payment terms, timeline, and ownership of the final work — so both sides know exactly what's being built.

When the site launches, you own it. Code, content, design — yours, with full rights and the source files handed over.
// 05

Hosting Handled For You

You don't need to learn what hosting is or manage server bills. We host your site on fast, reliable infrastructure and handle every renewal, update, and SSL certificate for you.

Care Plan: $50/month billed monthly via Stripe, or $500/year annually (save $100). Includes hosting, domain renewal, SSL, monthly backups, and security monitoring.
// 06

Ongoing Support

Need updates, content changes, or new features after launch? Optional maintenance retainers are available, or pay hourly only when you need us.

Maintenance retainer: $150–$400/month depending on update frequency. Ad-hoc work: $75–$95/hour. No retainer required to come back later.

Have questions about how a specific project would work? Ask us anything — happy to walk through the details before you commit to anything.
